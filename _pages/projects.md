This page highlights selected research programs. Published results are separated from ongoing methodological development.

## Magnetoelastic Heisenberg and XXZ models

**Status:** active / published and ongoing work

I study spin-1/2 isotropic and anisotropic Heisenberg models with deformation-dependent exchange interactions. The framework couples the magnetic subsystem to static lattice elasticity and vibrational thermodynamics, enabling systematic analysis of phase stability, first- and second-order transitions, and tricriticality.

Related publications:

- [Spin-1/2 quantum isotropic Heisenberg model with a magnetoelastic interaction](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMAG.2026.3679085)
- [Phase diagrams of spin-1/2 anisotropic Heisenberg model with magnetoelastic interaction](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7065953)

## Quantum-walk metrology

**Status:** published and ongoing extensions

This line of work studies how discrete-time and split-step quantum walks can be engineered for parameter estimation. The central questions include optimal probe preparation, attainable quantum Fisher information, multiparameter compatibility, and the role of walk topology in metrological precision.

Related publication:

- [Improving quantum walk metrology with split-step quantum walk](https://doi.org/10.1103/x6h2-4rbp)

## Analytical quantum-walk methods for many-body and statistical physics

**Status:** ongoing methodological research

I am developing quantum-walk-based analytical constructions for lattice statistical mechanics and interacting spin systems. The goal is to use the geometry and spectral structure of higher-dimensional quantum walks to organize propagation, operator ordering, graph embeddings, and asymptotic information in thermodynamic calculations.

Current targets include Heisenberg/XXZ systems and extensions toward Kitaev, J-K-Γ, XYZ, higher-spin models, information-geometric observables, and material-oriented case studies.

## Quantum walks on nontrivial geometries

My earlier work introduced and analyzed the **Möbius quantum walk**, in which an additional internal degree of freedom changes the topology and long-time distribution of a walk on a cycle. I have also studied asymptotic and limiting distributions for quantum walks with general U(2) coin operators.

Related work:

- [Möbius quantum walk](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8121/aa9796)
- [Asymptotic behavior and limiting distribution of quantum walk on cycles with general U(2) coin](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.13075)
