## Research experience

### Magnetoelastic spin-1/2 Heisenberg/XXZ systems

Current Ph.D. research on spin-1/2 anisotropic Heisenberg models with magnetoelastic coupling. The work uses an Oguchi/dimer mean-field treatment of the magnetic subsystem and evaluates the Helmholtz free energy including static-lattice, vibrational, and magnetic terms.

Current calculations include equilibrium strain, magnetization, phase boundaries, thermodynamic response functions, first- and second-order transitions, and tricritical behavior.

### Kitaev and hyperhoneycomb extension

Current research direction aimed at extending simple-cubic XXZ magnetoelastic models toward bond-dependent Kitaev-type interactions on the hyperhoneycomb lattice, with possible J-K-Γ generalizations and connection to β-Li₂IrO₃.

### Quantum information, metrology, and quantum walks

Research background in quantum information and quantum computation, including discrete-time quantum walks, quantum parameter estimation, quantum Fisher information, multiparameter estimation, and connections between quantum-information diagnostics and correlated quantum matter.
