## Current position

**PhD Researcher — Theoretical Condensed-Matter Physics**  
Department of Theoretical Physics and Astrophysics, Pavol Jozef Šafárik University in Košice, Slovakia

Research on quantum spin systems, magnetoelastic interactions, statistical mechanics, thermodynamics, and phase transitions. My dissertation work centers on spin-1/2 Heisenberg/XXZ models with coupled magnetic and lattice degrees of freedom.

## Research background

My earlier doctoral and research work was in **quantum information and computation**, with emphasis on discrete-time quantum walks, asymptotic dynamics, entanglement, and quantum parameter estimation. This background now informs my efforts to connect quantum-information concepts with many-body and statistical physics.

## Research projects and collaborations

I participate in theoretical quantum-magnetism research at Pavol Jozef Šafárik University and contribute to work on magnetic phase transitions, quantum spin models, and information-theoretic methods.
